WHY YOU NEED IT

Shopping online is a trust exercise. Stop guessing.

Listings are optimized to sell, not to inform. ShopBot AI reads the page the way a skeptical friend would — before you click "Buy Now."

Dual-Layer Multimodal AI

Reads visual screenshots and page metadata directly off your screen — combining what you see with what the page actually says.

Real Review & Sentiment Scraper

Cuts through fake and templated reviews to surface genuine customer pros, cons, and recurring product defects.

Seller Trap & Fee Detector

Flags hidden handling fees, non-returnable policies, and short return windows before you check out — not after.

100% Free & Serverless

Connects directly from your browser to Google Gemini using your own free API key. Zero tracking, zero middleman servers.

HOW IT WORKS

Four steps. No coding required.

Since ShopBot AI isn't on the Chrome Web Store yet, you'll load it manually — it takes about two minutes.

01

Download & unzip

Download the release file from GitHub and unzip the folder anywhere on your PC.

02

Open Chrome extensions

In Chrome, go to chrome://extensions/ and toggle on "Developer mode" in the top right.

03

Load unpacked

Click "Load unpacked" in the top left and select your unzipped folder.

04

Add your free API key

Click the ShopBot AI icon, paste your free Google Gemini API key — grab one at aistudio.google.com — and click Save.
